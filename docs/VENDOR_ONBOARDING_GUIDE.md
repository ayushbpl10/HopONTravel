## 1. Quick Onboarding & Authentication

### A. Accessing the Vendor Operations Hub
1. **Web Portal**: Visit [abtohghoomle.com/vendor-portal.html](https://abtohghoomle.com/vendor-portal.html)
2. **Mobile App**: Open the **Ab Toh Ghoom Le** mobile app and navigate to **Profile > Organiser Hub**.
3. **Sign In with Google**:
   - Click **"Vendor Sign in with Google"**
   - Authorize with your official Google account.
   - *Note for Testing*: You can click **"⚡ Test as Demo Vendor"** to explore the dashboard in read-only preview mode. To publish real trips or confirm bookings, sign in with your verified Google account.

### B. Setting Up Your Organiser Profile
Once signed in, click **"Edit Organiser Profile"** to configure your public brand:
- **Brand / Organisation Name**: e.g., *Sahyadri Trekkers Club*, *Himalayan Nomads*.
- **Contact Number & WhatsApp**: For booking notifications and urgent traveller calls.
- **Public Bio**: Highlight your safety track record, years of experience, and certifications.
- **UPI ID / Bank Handle**: For receiving direct participant payments.
- **Social Handles**: Add your Instagram handle (e.g., `@sahyadri_trekkers`) to build trust.

## 2. Listing & Publishing Your First Trip

You have three powerful ways to publish a trip on Ab Toh Ghoom Le:

### Option 1: AI WhatsApp Itinerary Importer (Recommended — 5 Seconds)
If you already draft trip announcements in WhatsApp groups:
1. Click **"✨ AI WhatsApp Import"** in the Vendor Dashboard.
2. Paste your raw WhatsApp text message into the box.
   > **Example Input:**
   > *"Rajmachi Fireflies Trek & Camping! Dates: 12-13 Oct. Price: Rs 1399 per person. Pickup: Dadar 10 PM, Thane 11 PM. Includes: Transport, Dinner, Breakfast, Guide. Excludes: Personal expenses. Difficulty: Moderate."*
3. Click **"Parse with AI"**.
4. The system will automatically extract and populate:
   - Trip Title & Category (Trek / Camp / Backpacking)
   - Price per person
   - Batch dates & capacity
   - Pickup locations & pickup times
   - Inclusions & Exclusions
   - Detailed Day-wise Itinerary
5. Review the preview and click **"Save & Publish Adventure"**.

### Option 2: 1-Click Popular Trek Templates
For popular routes across Maharashtra and India, use our verified templates:
1. Click **"⚡ Use Template"** dropdown.
2. Select from curated presets:
   - *Harishchandragad & Kokankada Trek* (₹1,299)
   - *Rajmachi Fireflies & Fort Night Trail* (₹1,399)
   - *Kalsubai Sunrise Peak Expedition* (₹1,199)
   - *Devkund Waterfall Secret Canyon* (₹1,499)
   - *Sandhan Valley Wonder Trek* (₹1,899)
3. Set your specific batch dates and click **"Create Trip"**.

### Option 3: Manual Custom Trip Builder
1. Click **"➕ Create New Trip"**.
2. Fill out the comprehensive trip creation form:
   - **Trip Title & Slug**
   - **Cover Image URL** (ImgBB, Cloudinary, or upload directly)
   - **Starting Location & Destination State**
   - **Trip Duration** (e.g., 2 Days / 1 Night)
   - **Difficulty Rating** (Easy / Moderate / Challenging)
   - **Price & Advance Booking Token Amount**
   - **Pickup Points**: Add name and time for each stop (e.g., Dadar TT Circle at 22:00, Vashi Toll Plaza at 23:15).
   - **Day-by-Day Itinerary**: Add structured bullet points for each day.
   - **Inclusions & Exclusions**: What's covered (meals, travel, first aid) vs traveller responsibility.
3. Click **"Publish Adventure"**. Your trip goes live immediately!

## 3. Managing Batches & Seat Inventory

Each trip can support multiple departure dates (batches):
- Navigate to your trip and click **"Manage Batches"**.
- Add upcoming weekend dates (e.g., *Batch 1: Oct 12-13*, *Batch 2: Oct 19-20*).
- Define the total seat capacity (e.g., 30 seats per bus).
- As travellers book online, seats automatically decrement. When seats reach zero, the batch shows **"Sold Out"** automatically, preventing overbooking.

---

## 4. Booking Management & Passenger Manifests

### A. Reviewing Bookings
- In the Vendor Hub, open the **"Bookings"** tab.
- Filter bookings by status: **All**, **Pending**, **Confirmed**, or **Cancelled**.
- View traveller name, phone number, booked seats, pickup location, and timestamp.

### B. Approving / Confirming Bookings
- When a traveller submits a booking, it appears under **Pending**.
- Verify their payment (via UPI screenshot or payment gateway notification).
- Click **"✓ Confirm"** to approve the booking.
  - The traveller receives an updated confirmation ticket.
  - Seat count is officially reserved.

### C. Passenger Manifests & Export
- **Download CSV**: Click **"📥 Export CSV"** to get a clean spreadsheet for your team and accounts.
- **Print Conductor Sheet**: Click **"🖨️ Print Manifest"** to generate a clean, printer-friendly passenger boarding checklist with pickup stops, contact numbers, and emergency contacts.

## 5. Live GPS Bus Radar (Trip Captain Mode)

During departure day, reduce frantic phone calls by broadcasting real-time bus location:
1. Open the **Ab Toh Ghoom Le Mobile App** on your phone (or vendor portal on mobile browser).
2. Select your active trip and tap **"Start GPS Radar"**.
3. Allow location permissions (foreground & background).
4. The system will broadcast your coordinates securely.
5. All travellers with confirmed tickets can tap **"Live Tracking"** on their traveller portal to watch the bus approach their pickup point on an interactive map.
6. When the trip ends, tap **"Stop Radar"**.

## 7. Safety & Compliance Guidelines

To maintain verified vendor status:
1. **Safety First**: Always carry a certified Wilderness First Aid kit and maintain at least 1 trek leader per 10-12 participants.
2. **Accurate Itineraries**: Ensure reporting times, inclusions, and difficulty ratings reflect actual ground realities.
3. **Leave No Trace (LNT)**: Enforce strict zero-littering policies on trails.
4. **Emergency Contact**: Ensure your organiser contact number is reachable 24/7 during active trip windows.
